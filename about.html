Our story

A neighbourhood café with roots in the rich coffee-growing highlands of Kenya.

Since 2019

From Nyeri, with love

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee. Founder Amina Otieno grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri and wanted to bring that flavour to the city.

Today the café supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month. We believe a great cup of coffee can bring people together.

“Every cup is a chance to share the warmth, care and remarkable flavour of Kenyan coffee.”— Amina Otieno, Founder
